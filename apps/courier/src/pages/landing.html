All that traffic, and no idea who’s behind it.

Agents can pass for ordinary visitors, so a shop often has no way to know if a real person is behind one.

We want to change that.

Courier gives each agent an ID, and PayPal vouches for the real person behind it, so a shop can trust who it’s selling to without ever learning your name.

It starts in your agent session.

Ask for dinner the way you’d ask anyone. Your agent finds it, tells you the price, and sends its courier out once you say yes.
