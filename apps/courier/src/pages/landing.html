Agents can buy now, so we’re turning them into couriers.

A courier works for someone and carries proof of it. Yours carries an ID tied to you, so shops know a real person sent it without seeing your name, and it only spends what you allow.

You’re still in charge once it’s out the door.

Your courier can bring helpers to compare prices and check stock, but each one carries its own ID and none of them can buy anything.

If you change your mind, one tap calls your courier back, and any helpers it brought stop with it.
